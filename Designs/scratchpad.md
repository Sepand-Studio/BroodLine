# Broodline — stakeholder pitch deck

Audience: internal stakeholders / publishing partners. ~10-12 min. 15 slides.
Title style: short topic noun-phrases, no punchline titles.

## Title sequence
01. Broodline (title)
02. Premise
03. Core Loop
04. The Splice
05. Hybrid Lineage
06. Wave Defense
07. The Mobile Gene Ark
08. Resource Pressure
09. Alliance Territory
10. The Apex Rally
11. Creature Cast
12. Counter Design
13. Monetization
14. Live Ops Cadence
15. Where We Are

Read back: premise → the loop and its three beats → the meta layer and its
pressure → the social layer → the cast and why it stays deep → business model
→ current state. A reader of titles alone follows it.

## Visual system
- Deck palette follows the game: violet #7a6ac0, teal #6ba7c0, amber #e8b34a,
  coral #e5867a, green #7cc492, ink #3f3a52, paper #f7f4fb.
- Baloo 2 for titles, Nunito for body — same as the game.
- Section rhythm: dark ink slides for section starts, paper slides for content,
  full-bleed screenshot slides for the five captured screens.
- Screenshots aspect-fit on contrasting ground, never overlaid with text.
