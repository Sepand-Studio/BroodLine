Shape language Three rules
Rule 01
Silhouette carries the role
Wide and low is a wall. Tall and narrow is ranged. Spindly is fast. Players should name the role before reading the card.
Rule 02
Hybrids add parts, never average them
A hybrid keeps one parent's body and bolts on the other's signature part. Muddy in-between blends kill the read and the collecting.
Rule 03
Growth exaggerates, never redesigns
Runt to apex is the same creature with mass moved outward — bigger head, heavier limbs, same profile. Players keep recognising their own animal.

What the matrix proves
All six founders stay relevant
Each founder hard-counters at least one raider, so no founder becomes the obvious skip. Pale was the risk — Screen and Chill are both mitigation, which counters nothing — so Courser exists specifically to make slowing the only answer.
Traits with no counter
Carapace, Litter, Regrow and Screen answer no specific raider — and that is correct. They are survivability and economy traits that make a team last, not traits that solve a threat. Every founder still owns one counter trait alongside them.
Wave design rule: never send two raiders countered by the same trait in one wave, or one perfect defender clears it.
Six founders give 15 first-generation pairings, and eight raiders on eight counter axes mean the meta cannot be solved with one team — the pressure that sends players back to breed. Growth stages for any of these live on Hybrid Growth; the splice pairing UI is Splice Chamber.
