## 2. The anchor purchase

**Permanent Double Regen — $9.99.** Charge regen improves from one per 25 minutes to one per 12.5.

This is what bible §8.5's trial converts to. The trial gives the effect free for 24 hours at the first charge wall; on expiry the player is offered two more days for Gene Shards or the permanent unlock. Every other purchase in the game is consumable; this is the one thing a player buys once and keeps, which is why it is the anchor.

It replaces **Mythic Lab Access** (48 hours of unlimited charges, $14.99), which is dropped from §8.3. Unlimited is self-limiting in practice — splicing consumes parents and a player runs out of creatures before charges — but selling an unlimited *anything* invites the reading that throughput is purchasable without bound, and the pack also sat outside the shard ladder. Register 6.6.

**Double Regen does not stack** with Geneticist Tier 4's 10% regen bonus multiplicatively; Tier 4 applies to the base, so a Tier 4 owner regenerates at 11.25 minutes, not 11.25 of a halved figure. The charge cap is unchanged by either.

## 3. Upper shard tiers

Above the $19.99 Geneticist's Vault, shards are sold directly rather than as packs:

| Price | Shards | Per dollar |
|---|---|---|
| $49.99 | 9,000 | 180 |
| $99.99 | 20,000 | 200 |

Direct shards, not themed packs, for two reasons. A $99.99 "pack" with a skin and fifty spins sets an expectation about the spending ceiling on day one, for an audience that does not exist until month six. And keeping the top tiers plain makes the ladder's monotonicity (economy §9) legible at a glance. Geneticist Tier 12's +15% applies here as everywhere.

**The $99.99 tier ships at launch but is not surfaced** — it appears only in the direct-shard list, never in a banner or event offer, until the first Apex Cup.

## 4. Offer cadence

Bible §8.4 says to cap FOMO events at a reasonable frequency. The number:

- **Never more than two timed offers live at once.** Always-on offers (Roulette spin packs, Recipe frames) do not count toward the two.
- **No timed offer shorter than 48 hours.** Countdown pressure below that is the pattern that earns the genre its reputation.
- **The free daily gift (§8.3) is never withheld** during an event, replaced by a paid offer, or made conditional.
- **Event bundles obey the ladder.** A bundle's shard-per-dollar never exceeds the fixed pack above it in price (register 6.5).

---

## 5. Advertising

**Rewarded video only.** No interstitials, no forced views, no ads in combat, on the splice screen, or in the confirmation path.

- +1 Splice Charge, three per day (bible §8.2)
- Small shard grants tied to daily objectives
- Introduced in session two, framed as a free lever — never as the alternative to a paywall

Century Games titles run zero ads. Rewarded-only is a deliberate departure: it gives non-payers a real third path alongside time and money, at the cost of some ARPDAU purity. The three-per-day cap is set against the charge economy, not against ad revenue — raising it dilutes the charge packs.

---

## 6. Regional pricing

Unaddressed until the localization plan exists. Two constraints it must satisfy when it does:

- The ladder stays monotonic in every currency.
- Double Regen stays the anchor in every market — its local price is set first and the ladder scales from it, not the reverse.

---

## 7. Guardrails

In addition to bible §8.7:

- Exactly one permanent purchase exists, and it is throughput
- Nothing sold is unlimited, for any duration
- Two timed offers maximum; none under 48 hours
- Upper tiers are direct shards, never themed packs
- Rewarded ads only, never in play

---

## 8. Open questions

1. **Is $9.99 right for Double Regen?** Priced by feel. It is the single number in the store most worth an A/B in soft launch.
2. **Rewarded ad cap at three.** Possibly low for the non-payer and right for the economy. Test five against charge-pack conversion before moving it.
3. **ARPDAU benchmarks** for the reference titles predate this work and need re-validating before they anchor any revenue model.
