# Broodline — The Mid-Game Arc

*Design spec, day 14 to day 90. Companion to bible §9.6 and §9.8.*

---

## 1. Why this document exists

Bible §9 is detailed through day 14 and then says "Apex Veins, Stake mechanics, garrison — requires an alliance and a mature roster." The genre's worst churn window is weeks three through eight, past anything onboarding covers. A player who survives the FTUE is standing at the edge of the part of the game nobody sequenced.

Six companions now put beats inside this window and each set its clock alone: the campaign's chapter boundaries, the economy's Core-tier timing and week-three tier-III checkpoint, the Collectors companion's step-down immunity, the region roster's band ladder, the live-ops calendar. **This document's job is to put them on one timeline and find where they collide.** It introduces no new mechanic.

---

## 2. The reference pace

Beats are keyed to **milestones**, not days. The days below are the reference pace for the **core archetype** (three sessions a day, Rich Deposit most weeks, in an alliance by week two); a faster or slower player hits the same beats on a different calendar, in the same order.

| 7 | Wave 8, chapter 1 done | 2 | Inner Reach | — | Immune | — |
| 10 | Wave 10 · Hauler unlocks (Drive 3) | 3 | Nudge toward the first gate | Alliance joined | Immune | — |
| 14 | Wave 16, chapter 2 done | 4 | Mid Reach | Member | Full immunity ends | — |
| 15 | Wave 17 · **Courser** | 4 | — | — | **Escort tutorial · step-down begins** | — |
| 18 | Wave 20 | 5 | First self-chosen relocation | First Splice Census at real stakes | Step-down | — |

| 35 | Wave 30 | 6 | — | First Stake Assault seen | — | — |

| 40 | Wave 33 · **Drift** · Deep Hauler unlocks (Drive 7) | 7 | — | — | — | — |

Two clocks bind this table. **Shards bind Core tiers** from tier 6 onward (economy §6: Core 8 at ~2.5 months for a core player), so a milestone is reached days or weeks before its tier is built. **Coverage binds waves** from chapter 4 onward (campaign §5), so the campaign slows to the Splicing Chamber's pace exactly as the Core timers lengthen. The two slow together, which is the staircase working.

## 3. Collisions

Where independently set clocks land in the same week, and what was moved.

**Week three carries too much.** Full immunity ends (day 14), Courser arrives (wave 17, day 15), the escort tutorial fires (day 15), the first self-chosen relocation happens (day ~18), and the economy's first tier-III checkpoint was "around week three." Four lessons in seven days. **Brood and the tier-III checkpoint are set at day 23**, the first day of week four, after the step-down completes. Week three teaches PvP and the map; week four teaches tier. The reference pace in campaign §2 and economy §7 is read accordingly: "week three" means the checkpoint is reachable by then, not that it lands then.

**Day 22 is a double threshold.** Full raid exposure and the end of chapter 3 fall on the same day. That is fine — chapter completion grants a guaranteed species (Pale) and a shard grant, so the day the player becomes fully raidable is also a day the game gives them something.

**The escort tutorial needs a gate.** Exposure is per gate (Collectors §6); a within-band run teaches nothing. The tutorial dispatches a Hauler through the nearest gate — from the Mid Reach at day 15, that is one hop out and one back — so the player watches a real 20-minute window with a real Transit Board listing, at the step-down's 10% loss cap. If the player is still in the Inner Reach at day 15, the tutorial routes Holdfast → Quillmoor → Greyspan: destination gate is exempt, so it uses Quillmoor as a pass-through. Bible §9.6 gains a day-10 nudge toward the first gate so this is rare.

**Garrison before Apex, not after.** Era-2 had first garrison (day 38) and first Apex participation (day 45) a week apart in the right order, and that order stands: a player who has already tied up one creature for the alliance understands the cost structure before an Apex run asks for escorts too.

**Second build slot lags its milestone by a month.** Wave 35 is reached around day 45; Core 8 is affordable around day 75. This gap is where a core player feels the shard curve for the first time, and it is intended — but the milestone screen must say *"Core 8 unlocked — 26,000 shards"* the day the wave clears, so the month reads as saving toward a known thing rather than waiting for an unknown one.

**Outer Reach and Bulwark arrive together.** Three lanes open at wave 41 and the Outer Reach is mostly three-lane. A player who can hold chapter 6 can hold Outer ground; the campaign is the practice room for the map, in that order, at about day 80. Apex extractions before that run through the alliance corridor and a Collector, not the Ark.

## 4. The phases

Four phases, one job each. The FTUE's rule was one new system per session; here it is **one new system per week through week five and none after.** A day-40 player does not need new mechanics; they need reasons to keep using the ones they have.

### Landing — days 14 to 22

**Goal: a reason to open the app that is not "the tutorial told me to."**

The step-down week (Collectors §11). Escort tutorial day 15, the first self-chosen relocation around day 18 — the first time the game asks the player to weigh the inverse rule (region roster §4) it has taught but not resolved for them — and the first Splice Census the player contributes to meaningfully. Courser is the campaign's contribution: the purest counter lesson, arriving while the player's attention is on the map.

The relocation is the phase's real content. It is placed inside the step-down on purpose: a player who has just chosen rich-but-exposed or poor-but-safe ground finds out whether they chose well at a 20% loss cap, not 40%.

### Depth — days 23 to 40

**Goal: sessions stop looking identical.**

Chapters 4 and 5 supply the variety: Brood at 25 (tier visibly matters for the first time), the second lane, Drift at 33 (the first breadth problem). Core 6 around day 28 is the first upgrade that cannot finish in a sitting; that is the beat where the Gene Lab stops being a menu and becomes a place things happen while the player is away.

Systems surfaced in this window, in order: Splice Roulette's tab at Core 5 (enough income to spend, and after the FTUE's ban on probability tables); the Trait Codex's full index once all twelve traits have been encountered (around wave 30); the first Stake Assault the player *sees*, as an alliance notification about a contest they are not in — social proof before participation.

### Commitment — days 38 to 75

**Goal: the first investment that outlasts a session.**

The highest-churn phase, because the game asks for something back: a garrisoned creature that cannot be used elsewhere, treasury contributions, a Core tier that takes a day.

**First garrison contribution is the moment to protect.** The game surfaces a **suggested contribution** — one mid-tier creature the player can spare, chosen by the same logic as the roster-wide unused-creature check — rather than asking the player to choose cold from a roster they do not yet know how to value. Getting this wrong reads as "the game asked me to give something up and I did not understand what."

First Apex extraction around day 45: as an escort on an ally's Convoy Rig, or with the player's own Deep Hauler once Drive 7 is built. Alliance tech's first branch around day 50 is when treasury contribution becomes worth making. The first revenge-token raid, whenever the first real loss lands, is the point at which raiding turns from something that happens to the player into something they do.

**Solo players make their real decision here.** A player unaffiliated at day 40 is telling the game something. Bible §6.10's promise — slower, not blocked — has to hold in practice: the Census's community layer, the Apex Cup and unclaimable Inner Reach must be visibly enough to keep a solo player through this phase. Metric 3 below is how that is checked.

### Arrival — days 75 to 90

**Goal: an identity the game can see.**

Core 8 and the second build slot around day 75 are the clearest evidence that investment compounds. Bulwark and three lanes at day 80 open the Outer Reach. By day 90 the economy's three archetypes should be visible as play patterns, not spending tiers:

| Archetype | Day 90 looks like |
|---|---|
| **Raider** | Drive and Harvest Array prioritised; Marks Shop regular; a revenge-token habit |
| **Builder** | Hatchery and Splicing Chamber prioritised; deep roster; high Lineage View engagement |
| **Optimiser** | Officer or active Stake contributor; Apex regular; Apex Cup competitive |

No archetype should be ahead on raw progression. What this phase adds is that the game *notices*: a recap or daily-objective set reflecting the player's actual pattern is worth more here than any mechanic, because it is the first moment the game can plausibly say "I see what kind of player you are."

## 5. Metrics

Bible §9.8 covers session one through day 14. This window's failure mode is slow rather than sharp — nobody quits mid-session; they stop coming back as often — so it needs its own instrumentation:

1. **Day 14 → 22 retention**, isolated, with the step-down loss cap as the variable. This replaces §9.8's single day-14 checkpoint with two: day 14 (full immunity ends) and day 22 (full exposure).
2. **Days to first tier-III counter.** Target day 21–25. Earlier and harvest sample rates are high; later and the Chamber curve is steep (economy §7).
3. **Solo vs allied retention from day 40.** If the curves separate by more than the category norm, §6.10 is a promise the game is not keeping.
4. **First-garrison completion rate**, and whether the suggested creature was accepted.
5. **Days from wave-35 milestone to Core 8 built.** Target ~30. Longer and the shard curve is biting early; shorter and Core is not the sink it should be.
6. **Session frequency by week**, not just retention.
7. **Archetype legibility at day 90.** If most players cannot be classified, the three archetypes are a modelling convenience, not a description of play.

---

## 6. Guardrails

- No new core system after week five; depth comes from existing systems
- Beats are keyed to milestones; days are a reference, never a trigger
- The step-down week and the tier-III checkpoint never share a week
- The escort tutorial always includes a real gate crossing
- The milestone screen always names the tier and its shard cost the day the wave clears
- First garrison contribution is suggested, never chosen cold
- Solo viability through day 90 is verified by retention data, not assumed

---

## 7. Open questions

1. **Suggested garrison, undoable once?** A one-time "take it back" lowers the first ask's stakes further. Lean yes; the garrison rules already allow withdrawal on cooldown, so this is copy and a shorter cooldown, not a mechanic.
2. **Archetype recap at day 90** is a product decision, not a metrics one. If the roadmap has no room for it, the archetypes stay advisory and metric 7 still runs.
3. **Roulette surfacing at Core 5.** Could be Core 4. The constraint is income, not tier; if core players are hitting Core 5 before ~2,000 shards a day of income, hold it.
4. **The wave-35-to-Core-8 month.** If soft launch shows it as a churn shelf rather than a saving period, the fix is the milestone screen's copy first, the Core 8 price second, never the milestone.
