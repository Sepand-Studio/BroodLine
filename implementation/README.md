# Implementation

Task plans and their outputs. Design and architecture live in `specs/`.

- `2026-09-08-phase0-entity-count-proof.md` — the render-budget proof that
  briefs the rig proof commission. **Signed off 2026-09-14**, by changing the
  reference device from A13 / 3 GB to **A14 / 4 GB** rather than by obtaining an
  A13: none was available. The margin that covered the gap was withdrawn with
  it, so the budget is the measured **10000 triangles**, not 7000. Two things
  survive the sign-off and are not waived — the re-run of this harness against
  real delivered meshes, and the rig proof itself, which moved to the art track
  because it is blocked on a commission rather than on engineering. Both now
  live in `specs/broodline_rig_proof.md` §8.3 and gate production species.
- `2026-09-09-phase1-engine-foundations.md` — the deterministic simulation core.

  format.
- `2026-09-11-phase4-backend-spine.md` — the backend spine: identity, the
  ledger, the config bundle, and the first Cloud Run service.

  **Read it before re-deciding anything it names, and before claiming this phase

  caller; and roughly fifteen defects found in the plan and the design, one of
  which was retracted and is recorded beside the retraction.
- `results/` — benchmark output. Logs and test XML are gitignored: they are
  regenerated by re-running a script. Five kinds of file are tracked instead:

  | Tracked | Why it is tracked |
  |---|---|
  | `entity-budget-*.csv` | Needs a particular physical device, off charge, for half an hour. The measured evidence behind the render budget |
  | `editor-replay.bin` / `-outcome.txt` | Needs a human to play wave 6 in the Editor and tap once. Phase 3's round-trip evidence at every dimension except IL2CPP and the device — `EditorReplayTests` re-simulates it on every run. Last captured 2026-09-14 under `0.2.0`, rally at tick 200. **Superseded by the same engine bump as the device capture below, and owed with it** — Phase 6 moved `SimVersion` to `0.3.0` and Phase 7 Task 1 moved it again to `0.4.0`, so this has now been owed across two phases |
  | `device-replay.bin` / `-outcome.txt` | Needs the physical device. Phase 3's done-when. **SUPERSEDED since 2026-09-15 — the capture is OWED and Phase 6 cannot close without it.** Last captured 2026-09-14 under engine `0.2.0` on an iPhone 15 Pro, rally at tick 205. Phase 6 Task 1 moved `SimVersion` to `0.3.0` and **Phase 7 Task 1 moved it again to `0.4.0`** (waves 1 and 2 needed a six-pocket lane), so both round-trip tests are bailing again and `TheTrackedCapturesAreCurrent` is RED — deliberately, by human ruling, because a skip is indistinguishable from success while a failure is visible. Do not re-baseline it green; re-capture it. The device tap window is **ticks 184–207** (the Editor's looser 184–240 is a different window, and the Phase 5 record conflated them). The marker is `TheTrackedCapturesAreCurrent`, and its polarity is the plain one: **green means the round-trip is being proven.** It replaced `TheDeviceRunIsSupersededAndIsNotReSimulated`, whose green meant the opposite. When the engine next moves past these artifacts it is the **only** test that goes red — verified in Phase 6 by weakening `SimVersion`, which gave 1 failed / 31 passed while both round-trip tests reported *passed* from their bail-out. There is still no `Skip` anywhere in the solution, so no count will ever report this for you |
  | `phase5-test-baseline.txt` | Reproducible, unlike the three above, and tracked for the opposite reason: it is the machine-diffable replacement for counts written into sentences.   | `phase6-test-baseline.txt` | Phase 6's equivalent, and the one place its counts live. **Its `gate.dotnet.failed` row is 1 on purpose** — `TheTrackedCapturesAreCurrent`, red until the device capture lands. Do not re-baseline it green |
