# Phase 10 — Raider cast art pass

The Frontier registry now contains the eight authored raiders plus the Sunder capstone. Runner retains Skirmisher/Courser size and charge cues. The Hauler family adds Breaker's fused upper plate, Bulwark's separate forward shield, and the Sunder carrying both at 1.5× size. Lash and Drift share the upright Lifter language, with a furled reach silhouette versus broad deployed membranes. Delver and Brood share a low segment chain, distinguished by digging forelimbs versus divisible-looking sections and hot seams. Every raider has a named `sk_kit` socket on its rig; this is presentation metadata and does not change the simulation.

`implementation/scripts/preview-frontier-raiders.py` compiles the actual C# builders through the offline Unity math adapter, checks finite channels, outward triangle winding, bone weights, bind poses, a 10,000-triangle ceiling and sampled moving/reaction poses, and writes `implementation/reviews/phase10-raider-cast.png`. The sheet has matching three-quarter/side views, normalized 40px silhouettes for shape recognition, and a separate shared-scale lineup for the intended size hierarchy. Current bodies range from 928 to 1,688 triangles. The companion export still passes unchanged.

Only Skirmisher, Courser and Lash exist in the current combat engine, so the other six are inspectable art definitions rather than newly playable enemies. The browser/offline view establishes geometry and proportion; Unity still needs to establish real lighting, animation, battlefield readability and performance in the final validation pass. The four-family body/kit asset sharing and combat telegraphs remain separate integration work.
